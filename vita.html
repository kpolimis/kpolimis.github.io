Projects

forest-confidence-interval (scikit-learn-contrib)

  • Led original development and first-authored the peer-reviewed JOSS publication (2017) for forestci (PyPI, scikit-learn-contrib) — a Python implementation of Wager, Hastie & Efron’s infinitesimal jackknife confidence intervals for random forests; contributes to ongoing development.

Wandarr — Travel Intelligence & Itinerary Platform

  • Built and launched wandarr, a production travel-intelligence PWA (FastAPI, PostgreSQL, React + Vite) that fuses Google Places POI discovery with OpenAIP drone-airspace data into ranked itineraries; self-hosted via Docker Compose behind Cloudflare with account-free invite links.

Multi-Sport Data & Analytics Platform

  • Engineered a production-grade sports prediction web app and FastAPI service serving calibrated win probabilities for NBA, NCAA, and NFL games; designed ETL pipelines ingesting from The Odds API, KenPom, and Sports-Reference into a Postgres backend with schema enforcement and lineage tracking.
  • Built an NBA data platform (nba_api, Airflow, dbt, Postgres) with medallion architecture, dbt schema tests, source freshness checks, and automated data quality validation.

Job Intelligence Platform

  • Architected a self-hosted, containerized job intelligence platform (Docker Compose + Ollama) that aggregates postings from multiple job boards daily, enriches each listing with a local LLM, and ranks by résumé fit using sentence embeddings and cosine similarity, delivering plain-language match narratives and a transparent Apply Priority heuristic on fully local infrastructure.
  • Developed a capability-matrix scoring layer that maps job requirements to structured skill ratings and generates per-match talking points; a self-service data product that converts a judgment-heavy screening process into a quantified, reproducible workflow with end-to-end data quality validation.

DroneVitals: UAV Telemetry & Operations Ecosystem

  • Built DroneVitals, a UAV post-shoot automation system pairing deterministic threshold agents with Claude Sonnet LLM agents under a SQLite-ledger orchestrator (idempotent, exactly-once delivery across subagents) — its SHA-256 + RFC-3161 forensic evidence layer produced a cryptographically verifiable claim packet used in a real Asurion warranty dispute; published to PyPI with 90%+ enforced test coverage.

Homelab AI Orchestration Platform

  • Designed and operate an AI-agent dispatch system that runs overnight coding sessions across 6 repos, synthesizes a prioritized work queue by 7 AM, and routes tasks by cost tier, from local Qwen (free) through DeepSeek to Claude, with a shared JSONL spend ledger and per-call budget caps; runs in production on a 92-container platform behind Cloudflare Tunnel, Authentik SSO, and Grafana/Loki observability, with a 5-model frontier panel that auto-escalates to Gemini Flash on model disagreement.
